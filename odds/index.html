Last Gallon: Box odds

Version 0.2.0 · every rate to 4 decimals · generated from the game's own roll code

Boxes are earned only: driving, GALLON RUN stars, horde nights held and first stage clears. Money and GOLD BARS never buy a box. What a box holds is fixed when it is earned. Boxes arrive sealed: you can peek inside any time, and the full contents are always shown before any GOLD BARS are spent on a box. GOLD BARS can only open it sooner.

These are the odds before any CREW lean or pity: no cards owned, no CREW pinned, pity counters at 0. The game's ODDS sheet shows each player's own odds from the same roll.

A ROADSIDE, HARDENED or ELITE stack is one of the player's CREW cards of that rarity half the time (the 4 pinned cards, or the 4 survivors who ran most recently), else even over every card of that rarity. LEGEND stacks are always even.

Pity

  • The 6th TOOLBOX-or-better box in a row without an ELITE line always has one.
  • The 40th TOOLBOX-or-better box in a row without a LEGEND brings the player's WISHLIST character (or the first LEGEND character not owned; in a GUN CASE a LEGEND gun not owned).
  • Pity is counted when a box is earned and never resets or expires.
  • LEGEND lines open once stage 3 has been cleared.

Which box you earn

Each source deals its boxes from a fixed, shuffled cycle of 100, so there are no bad streaks. At most 8 boxes a day from driving, runs and nights; past that the meter pays scrap instead. Paying never changes it. The first clear of a stage gives a GUN CASE (an ARMY CRATE at stages 5, 10 and 15), once per install.
