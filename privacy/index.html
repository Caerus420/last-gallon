Last Gallon: Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 4, 2026
Developer: Studio420, South Africa
Contact: s.koekemoer25@gmail.com

This policy explains what information the mobile game Last Gallon: Zombie Car Smash ("the game", "we", "us") handles, why, and what choices you have. It is short because the game collects very little.

1. The short version

  • You don't need an account, and we never ask for your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos or location.
  • Your progress is saved on your device. We run no servers that store your game data.
  • This version of the game contains no advertising, analytics or payment services and doesn't connect to the internet. It sends no information about you anywhere.

2. Information stored on your device

The game saves the following in its private app storage on your phone. Other apps can't read it, and it isn't sent to us:

  • Game progress and settings: scrap balance, vehicles and upgrades owned, stages unlocked, distance records, run statistics and your settings (for example touch controls, vibration, tilt steering, graphics quality).
  • Tutorial hints you have already seen, and the daily supply drop calendar.
  • A local gameplay log: a small file of game events (for example "run ended, 1,400 m, 32 zombies") with a random session number that changes every launch. It stays on your device and is not uploaded.

Backups. Android can back up app data to your Google Account (Android's backup setting) so your progress survives a new phone. Google runs that backup under your Google Account and your device settings. We have no access to it.

Deleting it. Choosing NEW GAME in the game resets your progress (your settings are kept). Uninstalling the game deletes all of the above from your device. You can also clear it in Android Settings > Apps > Last Gallon > Storage > Clear storage.

3. Device permissions

The game asks for no dangerous permissions (no location, camera, microphone, contacts or files) and no internet access. It uses:

  • Vibration, for optional haptic feedback (can be turned off in the pause menu).
  • Motion sensors (no permission needed), only if you turn on tilt steering. Sensor readings are used on the device and never stored or sent anywhere.

4. Children

Last Gallon contains cartoon-style violence against zombies and blood effects and is not directed at children under 13. It is not part of Google Play's Families programme. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the game, contact us and we will help delete it.

5. Sharing and selling

We don't sell your personal information. This version of the game doesn't send it anywhere, so there is nothing to share.

6. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the EU/UK GDPR or the California CCPA/CPRA), you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal data about you, to object to or restrict its use, to withdraw consent and to complain to your local data protection authority. The data described here never leaves your device, and you control it as described in section 2. For anything else, email s.koekemoer25@gmail.com. We will answer within 30 days.

7. Changes

If an update starts handling data in a new way (for example ads, purchases or analytics), we will update this page and the effective date above before that update is released. The in-game copy of this policy (PRIVACY POLICY on the title screen and in the pause menu) changes with it.
