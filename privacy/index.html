Last Gallon: Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 21, 2026 (version 0.2.0, "Survivors & Loot")
Developer: Studio420, South Africa
Contact: s.koekemoer25@gmail.com

This policy explains what information the mobile game Last Gallon: Zombie Car Smash ("the game", "we", "us") handles, why, and what choices you have. It is short because the game collects very little.

1. The short version

  • You don't need an account, and we never ask for your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos or location.
  • Your progress is saved on your device. We run no servers that store your game data.
  • The game contains no advertising and no analytics services.
  • Purchases are handled by Google Play. We never see your payment details. The game checks each purchase on your device: there is no game server, and nothing about your purchases is sent to us.

2. Information stored on your device

The game saves the following in its private app storage on your phone. Other apps can't read it, and it isn't sent to us:

  • Game progress and settings: scrap, GOLD BARS, vehicles and upgrades owned, your crew and their cards, boxes, stages unlocked, distance records, run statistics and your settings (for example touch controls, vibration, tilt steering, graphics quality).
  • Tutorial hints you have already seen, the daily jobs and the daily supply drop calendar.
  • Your answer to the SHOP's age question, kept only as an age range (see section 5).
  • Purchase records: what you own, what you spent GOLD BARS on, this month's and today's spending (for the spending limits in section 4), and the purchase confirmations Google Play returns, so a purchase is given once and one-time purchases can be restored.
  • A local gameplay log: a small file of game events (for example "run ended, 1,400 m, 32 zombies") with a random session number that changes every launch. It stays on your device and is not uploaded.

Backups. Android can back up app data to your Google Account (Android's backup setting) so your progress survives a new phone. Google runs that backup under your Google Account and your device settings. We have no access to it.

Deleting it. Choosing NEW GAME in the game restarts the road and the bunker; your settings, named characters, gun cards, boxes, GOLD BARS and purchases are kept, so you don't lose what you earned or paid for. Uninstalling the game deletes all of the above from your device. You can also clear it in Android Settings > Apps > Last Gallon > Storage > Clear storage.

3. Device permissions

The game asks for no dangerous permissions (no location, camera, microphone, contacts or files). It uses:

  • Vibration, for optional haptic feedback (can be turned off in the pause menu).
  • Google Play billing, to make purchases. Purchases go through the Google Play Store app on your phone. Google's billing library inside the game uses the internet and network state permissions to talk to Google Play about purchases; the game's own code sends nothing.
  • Motion sensors (no permission needed), only if you turn on tilt steering. Sensor readings are used on the device and never stored or sent anywhere.

4. Purchases

The game's SHOP sells GOLD BARS packs and two one-time starters (an ELITE card you pick, plus GOLD BARS). Every purchase is processed by Google Play under your Google Account, under Google's own terms and privacy policy. We never receive your card or bank details, name or email address.

  • Checked on your device. Google Play sends the game a purchase confirmation (the product, an order number and a purchase token). The game checks it on your phone and keeps it there, so each purchase is given once and the starters can come back with RESTORE PURCHASES. There is no game server: nothing about your purchases is sent to us.
  • GOLD BARS live in your save. Uninstalling the game or clearing its storage deletes them (unless Android's backup brings the save back). Starters can be restored; GOLD BARS bought before cannot.
  • No random paid items. Money buys only GOLD BARS packs and the starters, each with a fixed, stated content. GOLD BARS never buy boxes. Boxes are earned by playing, and what is inside a box is fixed when you earn it. Boxes arrive sealed: you can peek inside any time, and the full contents are always shown before any GOLD BARS are spent on a box. The odds are shown in the game and at Box odds.
  • Spending limits. Adults can spend up to US$120 a day. Players aged 13-17, and anyone who prefers not to give a birth year, can spend up to US$35 a month (US$70 at 16-17), and only inside the SHOP. Anyone can set a lower monthly limit under SPENDING in the SHOP: lowering it works at once, raising it waits 3 days. A calm check-in appears at US$100 and US$250 in a month. Amounts are the US dollar prices Google Play lists; your store shows your own currency.
  • Quiet times. The game offers nothing to buy in your first two sessions, during a drive, a run or a horde night, or after a lost night or a pulled-out run until your next win. There are no pop-up offers and no countdowns on offers.

5. Children

Last Gallon contains cartoon-style violence against zombies and blood effects and is not directed at children under 13. It is not part of Google Play's Families programme. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the game, contact us and we will help delete it.

The age question. The first time you open the SHOP (or tap a GOLD BARS price) the game asks for a birth year; you can choose not to say. The year itself is never saved or sent anywhere: the game keeps only an age range on your device, to apply the spending limits above. If the answer is under 13, the SHOP closes for 30 days and the game keeps only that date.

6. Sharing and selling

We don't sell your personal information. The game sends nothing about you to us. When you buy something, Google Play processes the payment under your Google Account to provide that service.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the EU/UK GDPR or the California CCPA/CPRA), you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal data about you, to object to or restrict its use, to withdraw consent and to complain to your local data protection authority. The data described here stays on your device, and you control it as described in section 2. Your Google Play purchase history is kept by Google under your Google Account (play.google.com, Payments and subscriptions). For anything else, email s.koekemoer25@gmail.com. We will answer within 30 days.

8. Changes

If an update starts handling data in a new way (for example ads or analytics), we will update this page and the effective date above before that update is released. The in-game copy of this policy (PRIVACY POLICY on the title screen and in the pause menu) changes with it.
